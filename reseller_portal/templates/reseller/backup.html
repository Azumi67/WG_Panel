Backup scope & recovery limits

The encrypted ZIP contains a manifest and an authenticated customer-recovery payload; it is not a database dump. Recovers deleted customers with their original keys and subscription links. Recreated peers return paused; enable them to install their configuration on the assigned local interface or node. Current plan limits, consumed traffic and elapsed timers remain protected. Existing customer access states are retained. Profiles and designs are restored only when missing and permitted. Old settings-only backups cannot recover deleted customers. Panel secrets, server configuration, payment records and other accounts are excluded.
